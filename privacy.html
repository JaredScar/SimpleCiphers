Privacy policy

Your message never leaves this page.

Simple Ciphers is a classroom tool for encrypting, decrypting, and brute-forcing a few classical ciphers. The work happens in your browser.

What stays on your device

Plaintext, ciphertext, keywords, and alphabet keys are processed locally by the script that ships with this site. They are not sent to a server, saved in an account, or written to a database by this project.

What this site does not collect

There is no account, no analytics tag, and no advertising. This page does not set tracking cookies, and it does not embed third-party ad scripts. A host you upload these files to may keep ordinary server logs of its own. Those logs are the host’s, not a feature of Simple Ciphers.

Fonts

The typefaces load from Google Fonts. Requesting them shares your IP address with Google. If that request is blocked, the page falls back to fonts already on your computer and the ciphers still run.

Links to other sites

Links to Wikipedia, the class directory, GitHub, and the author’s site leave Simple Ciphers. Those sites have their own policies.

Children

The bench does not ask for anyone’s name, email, or other personal information, and it is not directed at collecting information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to thewolfbadger@gmail.com.

Consent

Using the site means you understand that encryption runs in your browser, and that outbound links are outside this policy.
